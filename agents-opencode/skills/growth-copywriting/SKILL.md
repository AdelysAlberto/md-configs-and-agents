# High-Conversion Copywriting & Sales Persuasion Standards

Master guide for persuasive, high-conversion product copy, straight-line value propositions, and conversion funnel optimization.

## 1. Core Copywriting Invariants

1. **Relentless Straight-Line Focus**: Every headline, bullet point, and Call-to-Action (CTA) must move the visitor directly towards the conversion goal. Remove filler words and speculative waffle.
2. **Zero AI Clichés**: Strictly prohibit generic AI fluff (*"Descubre la revolucionaria plataforma...", "En el mundo digital de hoy...", "Lleva tu negocio al siguiente nivel..."*). Use concrete, visceral, authentic human language.
3. **Tripartite Delivery Standard**: Every conversion block must specify:
   - **Exact Copy**: Literal headline, subhead, and CTA text.
   - **Visual / UX Direction**: Placement, typography hierarchy, and visual anchor.
   - **Psychological Rationale**: The behavioral trigger addressing the customer's doubt.
4. **Action-Oriented CTAs**: Replace passive buttons (*"Enviar", "Aceptar"*) with first-person benefit verbs (*"Iniciar mi prueba gratuita", "Optimizar mis rutas ahora"*).

## 2. Persuasion Frameworks

### A. PAS (Problem - Agitation - Solution)
Ideal for hero sections and problem-validation blocks:
- **Problem**: Identify the visceral day-to-day frustration the user experiences.
- **Agitation**: Expose the hidden financial, time, or emotional cost of letting the problem fester.
- **Solution**: Introduce the product as the inevitable, friction-free relief.

### B. AIDA (Attention - Interest - Desire - Action)
Ideal for full landing page structures:
- **Attention**: Provocative, benefit-driven hero headline.
- **Interest**: Surprising data, architecture proof, or interactive demo.
- **Desire**: Social proof, case studies, and concrete features mapped to benefits (FAB: Feature ➔ Advantage ➔ Benefit).
- **Action**: High-contrast, single-focus CTA with zero risk (guarantee / no credit card required).

## 3. Landing Page Blueprint Structure

```markdown
# Landing Blueprint: [Product / Campaign Name]

### 1. Hero Section
- **Visual Anchor**: Clean UI mockup or live interactive preview (no generic stock photos).
- **Eyebrow Tag**: [Niche / Category defining tag]
- **Primary Headline**: [Direct, measurable benefit in < 10 words]
- **Subheadline**: [How it works + who it is for in 2 sentences max]
- **Primary CTA**: [Action-driven button text]
- **Social Proof Snippet**: [e.g. "Trusted by 1,200+ active couriers"]

### 2. Pain Agitation Block (Before vs After)
- Comparison table contrasting current manual misery with automated relief.

### 3. Core Feature Pillars (FAB Framework)
- **Feature**: What it is.
- **Advantage**: How it operates faster/better.
- **Benefit**: What the user actually gains (e.g. "Save 45 minutes every morning").

### 4. Risk Reversal & Final Push
- FAQ addressing the top 3 objections.
- Final high-visibility CTA banner.
```
