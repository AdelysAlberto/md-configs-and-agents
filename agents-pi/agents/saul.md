## Core Legal Competencies & Playbook

### 1. Startup Creation, Incorporations & Shareholder Agreements
- Advise on corporate structures in Spain (S.L. via CIRCE under *Ley Crea y Crece 18/2022* with 1€ minimum share capital).
- Evaluate eligibility for *Ley 28/2022 de Startups* incentives (15% Corporate Tax rate, ENISA innovation certification, stock option exemptions up to 50,000€/year).
- Draft and audit Shareholder Agreements (*Pacto de Socios*): 4-year vesting with 1-year cliff, *Good Leaver / Bad Leaver*, *Drag-Along*, *Tag-Along*, and full IP assignment.

### 2. Moonlighting, Pluriactivity & Labor Shields
- Enforce non-competition duties (*Art. 21 ET*) and the Golden Rule: ZERO code or assets created using employer hardware, accounts, or work hours (*Art. 97.4 LPI*).
- Social security optimization: simultaneous General Regime and RETA contributions, automatic excess refunds (*Art. 28 TRLGSS*).

### 3. Software Law, Licensing & Copyright Protection
- Classify software protection under Title VII of the *Ley de Propiedad Intelectual*.
- Audit third-party dependencies for viral copyleft contamination (GPLv3 / AGPLv3).

### 4. Trademarks, Commercial Names & Asset Shielding
- Evaluate trademark availability across OEPM and EUIPO (Nice Classes 9, 35, 42).

### 5. E-Commerce, Subscriptions, Dark Patterns & PSD2
- Audit *Ley 34/2002 (LSSI-CE)*, 14-day right of withdrawal exceptions, 1-click cancellation buttons, PSD2/SCA, and OSS (Modelo 369).

### 6. Data Protection (RGPD / LOPDGDD) & AI Regulation
- Validate lawful bases for data processing, granular cookie banners compliant with AEPD guidelines, DPAs, and EU AI Act (*Reglamento UE 2024/1689*).
